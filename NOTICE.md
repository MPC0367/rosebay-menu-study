# Notice — what is published here, and whose it is

Read this before reusing anything in this repository.

## This is an unsolicited design study

Rosebay Home Cooking & Café is a **real, trading restaurant** in Nong Nam Daeng, Pak Chong. This
page was made *about* them. It was not commissioned by them, it is not endorsed by them, it is not
their website, and it is not their ordering system. Nothing here may be presented as if it were.
The page is `noindex, nofollow` and carries that statement in Thai and English on its face.

## The photographs: whose they are, and on what footing each is shown

59 photographs are published here: one for each of the 58 items on the page, and the shopfront.
They are of three kinds, and every photograph says on the page which kind it is.

- **27 are Rosebay's own.** 15 the restaurant published itself on its Instagram
  (`@rosebayhomecookingcafe`) or Facebook, the shopfront among them. 11 are its own printed-menu
  photography, photographed from the printed menu book by Wongnai members. 1 is its own menu card,
  taken from its Google listing, where who uploaded the card was not recorded. **The restaurant's
  written permission is outstanding.** It has been neither given nor refused — it has not yet been
  obtained.
- **13 belong to other people.** They are photographs of Rosebay's food and drink found on Wongnai
  (7), Lemon8 (2), Pantip (1) and two review blogs (3). They are published here **without their
  owners' permission**. Each carries a caption naming the kind of source and saying that the
  restaurant did not post it and that its owner's permission has not been obtained. *For two of the
  13 the owner is not established.* Both were uploaded to the restaurant's Wongnai listing by one
  member, and whether that member is the restaurant or its photographer is an open question. They
  are recorded here as other people's until the restaurant answers it, and their captions say that
  ownership is not established in place of "not posted by the restaurant".
- **19 are sample images.** Where the studio has no photograph of an item at Rosebay that it can
  show, a photograph from a free-licence library stands in. For 15 of the 19 the studio found no
  photograph of the item at Rosebay. For three it found something it could not use: for two, only
  the restaurant's printed poster card inside a visitor's photograph, too small to show; for one,
  only photographs that more likely show a different drink. For one, the Thai iced tea, a Lemon8
  creator's photograph was found and is held back: the drink is in the corner of the slide opposite
  the mark its creator put on it, so no crop shows the drink and keeps that mark whole. **Sample
  images do not show Rosebay's food or drink, and none was taken at Rosebay.** Each is labelled on
  the page: "ภาพตัวอย่าง" / "Sample" on its
  thumbnail, and "ภาพตัวอย่าง · ไม่ใช่ภาพจากร้าน" / "Sample image — not from the restaurant" on its
  menu card and as the first words of its caption. Its file name begins `sample-`. Each is used
  under the licence listed under "Sample images" below. That licence is the permission, so nothing
  further is owed for these 19. The restaurant has not been asked whether it wants sample images
  shown for its menu.
- **No private person is named in the text** of the files of this repository as they stand: not in
  the words on the page, not in `menu.json`, not in this file or the README. The captions of the
  printed-menu photographs say "a Wongnai member" and no more, and the captions of other people's
  photographs name the platform only. *There is one exception in the text.* The creator of a sample
  whose licence requires attribution (CC BY and CC BY-SA, 7 of the 19) is named with the licence,
  because the licence asks for exactly that. The creators of the CC0, Unsplash-licence and
  Pexels-licence samples are not named. *The pictures themselves are a separate matter.* Three of
  other people's photographs show, in the picture, a mark their owner put there: a Lemon8 mark with
  its creator's handle on two, and an uploader's monogram on one. Those marks are kept whole, as the
  next point says, so a handle can be read in those two pictures. Earlier commits of this
  repository named three Wongnai accounts in the captions of the printed-menu photographs; that
  history has not been rewritten.
- **Marks inside a photograph are kept.** Inside the part of each photograph that is shown, no
  logo, watermark or other mark has been removed, cut through or retouched, whoever put it there.
  That covers the restaurant's own print on a cup, a bottle or a paper liner; a mark their owner put
  in the picture on three of other people's photographs (a Lemon8 mark with its creator's handle on
  two, and an uploader's monogram on one); and the Coca-Cola logo on the bottle in one sample.
- **In three photographs the crop leaves out something that sits elsewhere in the original frame**:
  a caption its photographer typed into the picture on one; a small watermark on one sample image,
  whose licence (CC0) asks for no credit; and the logo band of the restaurant's own menu card on
  one. Each of the three says so in its details on the page. That sample apart, a handle, watermark
  or signature that a photograph's creator or uploader put on it is never left out by a crop. A
  photograph that cannot be shown with it whole is not used, which is why the Thai iced tea shows a
  sample.
- Most of the photographs are cropped to the food or drink. In the eleven printed-menu photographs
  the crop leaves out the page headings and the names and prices printed beside the photographs,
  and in three of them it also leaves out a dish name or a price printed inside the photograph.
- **13 photographs were matched to their item by what they look like**, not by a caption or a label
  naming the item. Each says so in its details on the page.

**O2 Design Studio owns none of these photographs.** The 40 that show Rosebay's food,
drink and shopfront are published so the page renders as designed, on a `noindex` demonstration
page, by the studio owner's decisions of 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06. The 19 samples are used under
their licences. Those decisions are recorded in the studio's private project records, with the
provenance of every photograph.

For the 40, this is a known, accepted exposure, not an oversight. It resolves in one of two ways and
no others:

1. **Permission in writing** from the restaurant, and from each other owner, recorded in this file;
   or
2. **The photographs come out** of this repository — and out of its history, since a plain delete
   leaves every image in earlier commits.

## Takedown

If you are Rosebay Home Cooking & Café, or one of these photographs is yours — you took it, or you
are the creator of one of the sample images — and you want it removed, contact O2 Design Studio
through <https://o2-designstudio.com/>. It comes down on request. No reason is needed and none will
be asked for. A sample's licence allows this use; the offer stands for its creator all the same.

## The written content

Dish names, Thai spellings and options are transcribed from the restaurant's publicly posted menu
photographs (2021–2025), from articles and reviews, and from its own social posts. Five items are on
no menu photograph. Four of them are named in reviews, articles or the restaurant's own posts, and
the page labels them "Named only in reviews, articles or posts". For two of those four, the chocolate
tart and the mung bean pastry, the studio's notes also record the restaurant's own writing, seen in
photographs of the café: a chalkboard that names the tart, a printed card that names the pastry, and
price boards that list ขนมเปี๊ยะ and เปี๊ยะถั่ว (pastry). Those are boards and a card, not menu sheets,
and the label does not mention them. The fifth, the lemongrass drink
(น้ำตะไคร้), is recorded only in O2's own earlier design study of 29 August 2026; no menu, review,
article or post naming it has been found, and the page labels it "Recorded only in O2's earlier
design study". Prices are **historical** values read off those photographed menus; the restaurant
has not confirmed them. An item for which the studio has no confirmed price says "price to be
confirmed" rather than guessing. Five items say that although the restaurant has printed a price for
them, because the studio has not taken that figure into its records as the item's price. Each gives
the figure, says where it is printed and says it is unconfirmed. Those five are the figures the page
gives, not every figure the studio holds for an item with no confirmed price. Its records also hold:
prices reported in reviews and on a review blog for three other items (the mangosteen juice, the
chocolate tart and the mung bean pastry); figures written on the restaurant's own boards, noted from
photographs, for the chocolate tart and for ขนมเปี๊ยะ and เปี๊ยะถั่ว; a printed card for the pastry,
read only in part; and, for the longan drink, the same 80 in a visitor's review and on the
restaurant's own chalkboard in its post of 23 September 2026. Apart from that 80, none of those
figures is given on the page. The poster photographs cited for the four matcha drinks also show
figures for two items the page prices from older printed menus: Dirty Coffee at 120 and Hot
Chocolate at 130, where the page gives 100 for each and labels it historical. The page keeps the
older menu figure for those two. The longan drink (น้ำลำไย) is printed, as LONGAN JUICE at 80, on a
drinks page in a photograph uploaded to the restaurant's Wongnai listing in April 2024. Four matcha
drinks are
on the printed drinks posters on the café wall, read in visitors' photographs posted in 2026: ORANGE
MATCHA at 150, LONGAN MATCHA at 150, COCONUT MATCHA at 150 and DIRTY MATCHA at 130. Earlier versions
of this page said, wrongly, that the longan drink was named only in reviews and that no printed menu
gave it a price, and said of the four matcha drinks that no menu the restaurant printed carried a
price, without mentioning the posters. Nothing in the
written content is invented: where the sources are unclear, the page says so. The sample images are
the one thing on the page that does not come from Rosebay, and each is labelled as a sample.

The restaurant's name and marks are its own.
